Growth charts

Plot a child's measurements against the four growth references paediatric practice actually uses — WHO, IAP 2015, Fenton for preterm infants and INTERGROWTH-21st. Each returns the percentile and Z-score for weight, length and head circumference, and exports the plotted chart as a PDF for the record.
