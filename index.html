What's inside

Everything you reach for, in one place.

PediAid bundles the calculators, scores, charts and reference data that paediatricians and neonatologists juggle across tabs, books and bookmarks — designed around how paediatric care actually flows.

Clinical Calculators

Eighteen+ tools — from GIR and maintenance fluids to blood-gas, BSA, CGA/PMA and Schwartz eGFR — with visible formulas and safety bands.

Jaundice Assessment

Two complete pathways: AAP 2022 hour-specific thresholds for ≥35 weeks, and NICE CG98 graphical thresholds for 23 – 38+ weeks preterm.

Neonatal scores (Apgar, Ballard, Sarnat, Thompson, Downes, Silverman-Anderson, LATCH, POFRAS) alongside a paediatric library grouped by system — PEWS, Westley croup, Kawasaki, PECARN, Child-Pugh, PUCAI, DSM-5 screens and more.

Get it on Google Play Live now Download on the App Store Live now
📱 A note for iPhone & iPad users. Every major feature works exactly as it should on the App Store version. Apple's rules do restrict what a clinical app may present, so a small number of features — a few of the dose-calculation tools among them — are limited in the iOS build. If you ever need one of those, the web app carries no such limits: it runs fully in Safari, and once you add it to your Home Screen it behaves like an installed app.

For qualified clinicians. PediAid is a clinical aid only. Calculations and reference data must be verified against the patient's clinical context, the source guideline and your local protocols before any treatment decision is made.
