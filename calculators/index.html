Paediatric and neonatal calculators

Bedside calculators for paediatric and neonatal practice — fluids and electrolytes, nutrition, respiratory support, jaundice, cardiology and emergency dosing. Every calculator shows the formula it used and the reference it came from, so the number can be checked rather than trusted blindly, and all of them work offline in the app.
