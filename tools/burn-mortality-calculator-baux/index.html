Revised Baux Score Calculator

Burn Mortality Prediction

Open in PediAid → ← All tools

1 What is the Revised Baux Score Calculator?

The Revised Baux Score Calculator estimates predicted mortality risk after a burn injury from three inputs: %TBSA burned, patient age, and presence of inhalation injury. It can be opened pre-filled with %TBSA carried over from the app's Parkland Formula calculator. The resulting score is mapped to five mortality risk bands, from Low (under 5% predicted mortality) to Near 100% Mortality, each with a brief clinical description.

2 When to use it

  • Estimating mortality risk for a patient after a burn injury, using TBSA, age, and inhalation injury status
  • Triaging the level of care needed — for example standard burn unit versus burn ICU versus a more palliative-focused approach — based on the predicted mortality band
  • Following on from a Parkland Formula fluid resuscitation calculation, using the same TBSA value
  • Communicating burn severity and prognosis in a standardized, reproducible way across a care team
  • Identifying patients where inhalation injury meaningfully changes the risk estimate, since it adds a fixed point value to the score

3 Formula & method

Revised Baux Score = TBSA (%) + Age (years) + (17 x R), where R = 1 if inhalation injury is present and R = 0 if it is not, exactly as stated in the app. The resulting score is then categorized: below 70 = Low Mortality (<5% predicted), 70-90 = Moderate Mortality (10-50% predicted), 90-110 = High Mortality (50-75% predicted), above 110 = Very High Mortality (>90% predicted), and above 140 = Near 100% Mortality.

4 Frequently asked questions

What does the 'R' in the Revised Baux formula stand for?

R represents inhalation injury status — the app sets R = 1 if there is an inhalation injury, adding 17 points to the score, and R = 0 if there is none.

Can I carry over my TBSA from the Parkland Formula calculator?

Yes — this calculator can be opened pre-filled with the %TBSA already entered in the Parkland Formula calculator, so you only need to add age and inhalation injury status.

What mortality percentage corresponds to a score of 100?

A score of 100 falls in the app's 90-110 band, labeled High Mortality with 50-75% predicted mortality.

Is age the only demographic factor in this score?

Within this tool, yes — the Revised Baux formula as implemented only takes TBSA, age, and inhalation injury status; it does not add other comorbidities.

What does a score above 140 mean?

The app labels this 'Near 100% Mortality', describing it as extremely high mortality with near-certain death in most populations.
