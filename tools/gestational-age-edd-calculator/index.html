Gestational Age & EDD Calculator

EDD, GA & Antenatal Dates

Open in PediAid → ← All calculators

1 What is the Gestational Age & EDD Calculator?

The Gestational Age & EDD Calculator estimates a pregnancy's due date and current gestational age using five different dating methods — last menstrual period (LMP), dating ultrasound, a known EDD, IVF/ART embryo transfer date, or a known conception date. Once a due date is set, it tracks trimester progress, shows expected fetal development for the current gestational week, and generates a calendar of antenatal surveillance milestones calculated forward from the estimated LMP.

2 When to use it

  • Establishing an estimated due date (EDD) at a first antenatal visit from LMP, a dating ultrasound, or an already-known EDD
  • Dating a pregnancy conceived via IVF/ART using the embryo transfer date and embryo age (Day 5, Day 3, or Day 0)
  • Tracking gestational age, trimester, and days remaining to term through pregnancy
  • Planning the timing of routine antenatal tests and interventions (NT scan, anomaly scan, glucose screening, Tdap, Rho(D) immunoglobulin, GBS screening)
  • Reviewing expected fetal development at a specific gestational week
  • Calculating gestational age on any specific calendar date, such as a past or future visit

3 Formula & method

The calculator offers five dating methods, each with its own formula shown in the app: LMP method — Corrected LMP = LMP + (cycle length − 28), then EDD = corrected LMP + 280 days; Ultrasound method — EDD = scan date − GA at scan + 280 days; Known EDD method — LMP = EDD − 280 days (back-calculated); IVF/ART method — EDD = embryo transfer date + 261 days for a Day 5 blastocyst transfer, + 263 days for a Day 3 transfer, or + 266 days for a Day 0 (fresh retrieval) transfer; Conception-date method — EDD = conception date + 266 days, LMP = conception date − 14 days. From the resulting LMP, the app also builds a calendar of antenatal windows (e.g. NT scan 11w0d–13w6d, anomaly scan 18w–22w, glucose screen 24w–28w, Rho(D) at 28w, Tdap 27w–36w, GBS screen 36w–37w6d) plus condition-specific antenatal-testing start points such as 32 weeks for monochorionic-diamniotic twins, poorly controlled diabetes, or a prior stillbirth, per the app's own list.

4 Frequently asked questions

What dating methods does this EDD calculator support?

Five: last menstrual period (LMP) with cycle-length correction, dating ultrasound, a previously known EDD, IVF/ART embryo transfer date, and a known conception date.

How does IVF/ART dating differ from LMP dating?

EDD is calculated directly from the embryo transfer date, adding 261 days for a Day 5 blastocyst transfer, 263 days for a Day 3 transfer, or 266 days for a Day 0 (fresh retrieval) transfer.

What if my cycle length isn't 28 days?

The LMP method corrects for this: Corrected LMP = LMP + (cycle length − 28), before adding 280 days to get the EDD.

What antenatal milestones does the calculator flag?

It generates a calendar of common surveillance windows — NT scan, anomaly scan, glucose screening, Rho(D) immunoglobulin, Tdap vaccination, and GBS screening — calculated from the estimated LMP. The app describes this as a workflow aid to be used with clinician judgment and current practice guidance.

What guidelines does the tool reference?

The app's own disclaimer cites ACOG Practice Bulletins, RCOG Green-top Guidelines, NNF India, and IAP India Guidelines, and states results should be used with clinical judgment.
