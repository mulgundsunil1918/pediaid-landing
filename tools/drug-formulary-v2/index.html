1 What is the PediAid Drug Formulary 2.0?

PediAid Drug Formulary 2.0 is a curated pediatric and neonatal drug reference organized into two sections: a live Neonatology formulary of 199 drugs derived from Neofax with India-specific brand names and gestational-age-band dosing, and a Paediatrics formulary (currently in beta) of 478 drugs derived from the Harriet Lane Handbook. Per the app's own description, entries have been cross-checked against the WHO World Model Formulary for Children (WMFc), NNF Clinical Practice Guidelines, the AAP Red Book, DailyMed, and the IAP Drug Formulary. It is described as a curated, copyright-safe formulary built from public sources, and users are instructed to verify every dose against local protocol before use.

2 When to use it

  • Looking up neonatal drug dosing by gestational-age band, including India-specific brand names
  • Referencing pediatric drug information derived from the Harriet Lane Handbook
  • Cross-checking a drug's dosing against multiple named public formulary sources in one place
  • Preparing to prescribe or verify a pediatric or neonatal medication order against a curated reference
  • Distinguishing between the fully authored Neonatology section and the beta, auto-extracted Paediatrics section

3 Formula & method

This is a navigational hub rather than a calculator. It presents two formulary sections as tiles — Neonatology (199 drugs, Neofax-derived) and Paediatrics (478 drugs, Harriet Lane-derived, labelled BETA) — that a user taps into to browse or look up individual drug entries.

4 Frequently asked questions

What sources is Drug Formulary 2.0 cross-checked against?

Per its own banner text, entries are cross-checked against WHO WMFc, NNF CPG, AAP Red Book, DailyMed, and the IAP Drug Formulary.

How many drugs are in the Neonatology section?

199 drugs, described in-app as Neofax-derived with India brand names and gestational-age-band dosing.

How many drugs are in the Paediatrics section, and is it complete?

478 drugs, Harriet Lane-derived; the app labels this section "BETA" and "Auto-extracted, awaiting full authoring."

Is Drug Formulary 2.0 meant to replace my hospital's protocol?

No — the app's own banner instructs users to verify every dose against their local protocol before use.

What does "GA-band dosing" mean in the Neonatology formulary?

It refers to dosing organized by gestational-age band, as stated in that section's own subtitle.
