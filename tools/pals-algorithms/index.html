PALS Algorithms

Pediatric Advanced Life Support

Open in PediAid → ← All tools

1 What is the PALS Algorithms?

PediAid's PALS Algorithms screen is described in-app as a way to 'tap any algorithm to open it directly in the PDF viewer' for Pediatric Advanced Life Support. Rather than reimplementing each algorithm as interactive steps, it acts as a table of contents into a bundled PALS reference PDF, jumping straight to the correct page for whichever algorithm is selected.

2 When to use it

  • Quickly opening the correct PALS algorithm page during a pediatric emergency such as cardiac arrest, bradycardia, or tachycardia
  • Reviewing the systematic approach or post-arrest care algorithms during training or a case debrief
  • Locating the foreign body airway obstruction sequence for a choking infant or child
  • Confirming length-based resuscitation guidance mid-code
  • Refresher review of shock recognition and post-ROSC management between courses

3 Formula & method

The screen lists 10 named algorithms, each mapped to a specific page number in a bundled PALS PDF: Pediatric Cardiac Arrest, Bradycardia with Pulse, Tachycardia with Pulse, Septic Shock, Management of Shock After ROSC, Length-Based Resuscitation, PALS Systematic Approach, Post-Cardiac Arrest Care, Child Foreign Body Airway Obstruction, and Infant Foreign Body Airway Obstruction. Tapping an entry opens the PDF viewer directly at that algorithm's page rather than presenting a separate interactive tool.

4 Frequently asked questions

What algorithms are included in PediAid's PALS section?

Pediatric Cardiac Arrest, Bradycardia with Pulse, Tachycardia with Pulse, Septic Shock, Management of Shock After ROSC, Length-Based Resuscitation, PALS Systematic Approach, Post-Cardiac Arrest Care, Child Foreign Body Airway Obstruction, and Infant Foreign Body Airway Obstruction.

Does tapping an algorithm open a separate calculator?

No -- it opens the bundled PALS algorithm PDF directly at the relevant page through the app's PDF viewer.

Is there a foreign body airway obstruction algorithm for infants specifically?

Yes -- the list includes separate entries for Child Foreign Body Airway Obstruction and Infant Foreign Body Airway Obstruction.

Can I jump straight to post-cardiac-arrest care guidance?

Yes -- 'Post-Cardiac Arrest Care' is a direct entry in the list, opening straight to its page in the PDF.
